My Work in Practice

What I’ve done as a barback at Fair Game and Ambassadors Club House in London.

Keeping the bar ready for service

I restocked glassware, ice, garnishes, and supplies so bartenders had what they needed during busy shifts. This meant paying attention to what was running low and replenishing it before it interrupted service.

Checking deliveries carefully

I checked incoming stock for accuracy, quality, and freshness, and reported shortages or damaged items promptly so the team could address them.

Supporting colleagues under pressure

I kept stations clean, organised, and stocked while balancing competing tasks. My role was to support bartenders behind the scenes so they could focus on serving customers.

What This Experience Has Taught Me

Notice the details, keep track of priorities, and act when something needs attention. These are habits I’ve built through day-to-day work in hospitality.
